What each module does for you

You never open a terminal or choose a model. Just answer in plain words.

AI Advisor

Turns your need + your hardware into the best model and setup — no guesswork. It reads what you want your AI to do and what your computer can run, then ranks open models by quality, speed and space. You never pick a model yourself — the best fit is chosen for you.

Set up your AI →

Auto-Deploy

Installs the runtime, model, RAG and UI automatically — no command line. One installer downloads the model, sets up the runtime and knowledge pipeline, and opens a ready-to-use web UI. From download to first answer takes about 20 minutes.

See the installer →
🎛️

Control Center

Manage models, knowledge, API and status in one screen. Swap models, add or remove knowledge files, and manage API keys without touching a config file. Run status is always visible, so you know your AI is healthy.

Open Control Center →
📚

Knowledge / RAG

Drag in PDF, Word, Excel — answers cite the exact source. Drop your files in and they are indexed automatically; nothing to configure. Every answer points to the file and page it came from, so you can verify it in one click.

Try it live →

Teach My AI

Your corrections train it over time — it gets more accurate the more you use it. Tap 👍 or 👎 on any answer and your feedback becomes training data. Week by week, your AI learns your terms, your files and the way you want answers.

Try 👍/👎 feedback →

Model Registry

Tracks open models, licenses, hardware needs and compatibility. New open models ship every week — we test and catalog them so recommendations stay current. When a better fit for your hardware appears, you will know.
