THE STEPS

What actually happens

Here's each step in detail — what you do, and what you see on screen.

01

Describe your need

Open the build wizard and type one sentence about what you want — or pick a ready-made template. That's all we need to get started.

02

Check your computer

Answer two quick questions about your computer — graphics card and memory — with plain-word hints, nothing to research. Automatic detection ships with our desktop app.

03

Get a recommendation

Your plan appears as simple quality / speed / space meters. No model names to decipher — we pick the best open model for you.

04

Install or deploy

Download the guided installer and run it — nothing to type — about 10–25 minutes to your first answer. If your computer isn't enough, you get a cloud deployment guide instead.

BEHIND THE SCENES

What you never have to touch

These are the parts that stop most people. They're our job, not yours.

🧭

Models

Which model, what size — the AI Advisor decides from your need and your hardware. You just see the result. Fine-tuning (Teach My AI) is coming soon.

⚙️

Environments

Docker, drivers, runtimes — the words that scare people off. The installer sets them all up automatically; you never see a command line.

📚

RAG plumbing

Chunking, embeddings, vector stores — the machinery behind asking your files questions. We wire it all up — see cited answers in the live demo today; dragging your own documents in is coming soon.

FAQ

Common questions

Short answers, in plain words.

Do I need a GPU?

Local mode needs an NVIDIA GPU. If you don't have one, you still get a full recommendation — plus a cloud deployment guide instead of an installer.

Is my data private?

In local mode, your files and questions never leave your device — it even works offline.

What if I get stuck?

The wizard explains every step in plain words before anything happens. And during beta you can reach out to us anytime — we read everything.
