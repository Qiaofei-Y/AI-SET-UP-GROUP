💻 Local

Recommended for private data
  • Highest privacy — data never leaves
  • Free after setup · works offline
  • Needs an NVIDIA GPU
  • Nothing to type — a progress window does the work
  • ● Works today Guided setup powered by Ollama · ~15–30 min, mostly the model download
  • ◷ Coming soon True one-click signed installer — ships with our desktop app

☁️ Cloud

When your computer isn't enough
  • No local GPU needed
  • Accessible from anywhere
  • Pay per use · data leaves device
  • ● Works today Step-by-step guide for a US cloud GPU · 30–60 min, assumes basic terminal comfort or a helper
  • No hardware to maintain
  • ◷ Coming soon One-click deploy with your own Lambda account — you pay Lambda directly · Part of Pro, free during beta

🔗 Hybrid

Best of both (Pro)
  • Local model for private answers
  • Cloud for heavy or complex tasks
  • You choose what stays private
  • You get both setups today — automatic routing arrives with Pro
  • Part of Pro — free during beta
