Privacy Policy

Draft v2026-08-25
DRAFT — Build My AI is a free, non-commercial open-source project. This policy describes our data practices accurately; it is provided in good faith and will be refined as the project evolves.

THE SHORT VERSION

Your AI runs on your computer. Your documents, knowledge base and chats never reach our servers — by architecture, not by promise. We collect only what an optional account needs, plus strictly anonymous, opt-in install statistics that structurally cannot contain your content. We never sell your data.

1. What we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, optional company name, and your password — stored only as a salted cryptographic hash (PBKDF2), never in plain text.
  • Opt-in anonymous install statistics: hardware tier (GPU class, memory size), chosen template and model, install success or failure, duration, and a fixed error code. These fields are validated against a strict whitelist of enumerated values — free text is rejected by the server, so your content structurally cannot end up here.
  • Feedback ratings: a thumbs-up or thumbs-down plus the template and model name. No message content, ever.

2. What we never collect

The product's core promise is that your AI is private. We never collect, transmit or store: your documents or knowledge-base contents, your chat questions or the AI's answers, your file names, or your folder structures. The installed product runs on your machine and answers your questions locally.

3. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and provide the services you sign up for.
  • To send transactional emails you request, such as email verification and password resets.
  • To improve installation success rates and model recommendations, using only the anonymous statistics described above.
  • We do not use your information for third-party advertising, and we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

4. How information is stored

Identity data and anonymous statistics live in physically separate databases, so anyone auditing the statistics store finds no identity fields in it. Passwords are salted PBKDF2 hashes; login sessions are stored only as one-way hashes, so a stolen database yields no usable credentials. Data in transit is protected by TLS on our production domain.

5. When we share information

  • Service providers who process data on our behalf: website hosting and, if you use account email features, an email delivery provider. They may use it only to provide their service to us.
  • Legal requirements: we may disclose information if required by law, subpoena or court order.
  • We never sell personal information.

6. Your rights (including California / CCPA)

Export and deletion are self-service: in your Control Center (Account card) you can download a full copy of your data as JSON and permanently delete your account — deletion erases your records at the file level, immediately. California residents additionally have the right to know what categories we collect (listed above in full), the right to opt out of sale or sharing (we do neither), and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. For corrections or anything else, contact us using the details in section 10; we will verify your request via your account email.

7. Retention

Account data is kept while your account exists. Self-service deletion erases it immediately and at the file level (freed storage is zeroed and compacted); only records we must keep for legal compliance survive. Anonymous statistics contain no identity and are retained in aggregate.

8. Children

The service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page with a new version date. For material changes we will notify account holders by email before the changes take effect.

10. Contact

Privacy requests and questions: email us at michael.yan@purehd.com, or open an issue on the project's GitHub repository. Maintained by the Build My AI open-source project.
